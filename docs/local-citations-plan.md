# Zexa Media Local Citations Plan

## Current Status

Website NAP consistency is complete. The next local SEO priority is to make the same business identity appear consistently on trusted map platforms, Indian directories, social profiles, and legitimate partner references.

Local citations should support discoverability for branded, local, and service-intent searches such as:

- Zexa Media
- Zexa Media Vizag
- digital marketing agency Vizag
- performance marketing agency Visakhapatnam
- Google Ads agency Vizag
- Meta Ads agency Vizag
- lead generation agency Vizag

## Why This Matters

Local search systems use clear business information, relevance, distance, and prominence signals to understand whether a business should appear for a local query. Consistent citations help search engines and AI search systems connect Zexa Media to the same real-world entity across the web.

This plan avoids risky shortcuts. Do not create fake listings, fake reviews, duplicate profiles, fabricated backlinks, or inflated claims.

## Partner Mention Request

Use this only for real partners or clients where a public mention is accurate:

```text
Hi, we are improving Zexa Media's official business profiles and local SEO consistency. If it is accurate for your website, could you add a simple partner or marketing support mention linking to https://zexamedia.in/?

Suggested line:
Marketing support by Zexa Media, a growth marketing agency in Visakhapatnam.

Please use this only if it genuinely reflects our working relationship.
```

## Review Request Rule

Ask for reviews only from real clients or partners after real work has been delivered. Do not offer rewards for reviews, do not ask for only positive reviews, and do not create reviews internally.

Suggested message:

```text
Hi, thank you for working with Zexa Media. If our work helped your business, could you please share an honest Google review? It helps other business owners in Vizag understand what working with us is like. Please write only your genuine experience.
```

## Weekly Maintenance Cadence

- Check Google Business Profile edits, messages, reviews, and photo updates.
- Check Bing Places and Apple Business Connect for pending verification issues.
- Update the tracker with live listing URLs and status.
- Make sure the same phone, address, website, and business name appear everywhere.
- Respond professionally to real reviews.
- Add new real photos or posts when there is useful business activity.
- Remove or correct duplicate, outdated, or wrong listings.

## Do Not Do

- Do not add keywords to the business name.
- Do not create duplicate profiles for the same business location.
- Do not use a fake or virtual address.
- Do not buy fake reviews.
- Do not create fake client results or fake case studies.
- Do not submit to low-quality spam directories only for backlinks.
- Do not use inconsistent phone numbers or old emails.
- Do not copy promotional pricing into profiles where it violates platform rules.
- Do not claim awards, client logos, or partnerships without permission.
