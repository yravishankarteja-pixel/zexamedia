# Free Remote Audit UX

## Current Status

The Free Audit page now supports worldwide visitors, not only Vizag/local buyers. It explains how a business can book an audit remotely and what Zexa Media reviews before making recommendations.

## Problem

Global visitors may hesitate if the audit page looks local-only or unclear. They need to know:

- The audit can happen online.
- View-only access or screenshots are enough for diagnosis.
- Zexa will not change campaigns without permission.
- The output is a clear next-step summary.
- The recommendation is based on the business model, not location.

## Exact Fix Implemented

Updated the Free Audit page with:

- Global page metadata.
- Worldwide hero positioning.
- Remote audit trust cards.
- Access and asset checklist.
- Audit deliverables checklist.
- Updated audit process copy.
- Worldwide schema area served.

Updated the audit form with:

- Performance marketing.
- Lead generation.
- SEO / AI search visibility.
- Remote marketing audit.
- Not sure yet budget option.

## UX Reasoning

The page now reduces uncertainty before the form. A worldwide visitor can understand what to share, what happens next, and what they will receive before booking.

## Verification Checklist

- Page does not confine the audit to Vizag.
- Remote audit workflow is clear.
- Access expectations are permission-safe.
- Form options match global service pages.
- No guarantees or fake claims are used.

## Next Improvements

- Add a thank-you page after form submission.
- Connect the form to email, CRM or WhatsApp automation.
- Add a downloadable audit preparation checklist.
- Add real testimonial or review sections only after verified feedback exists.
