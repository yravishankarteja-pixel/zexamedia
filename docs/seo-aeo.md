🔍 CHAPTER 01 — SEO vs AEO — What Zexa Media Needs to Rank For

Understanding SEO and AEO for a Growth Marketing Agency

SEO (Search Engine Optimisation) helps your website rank on Google when someone types a query. AEO (Answer Engine Optimisation) targets voice search, Google's AI Overviews, featured snippets, and AI chatbot answers (ChatGPT, Perplexity, Gemini). For Zexa Media, both work together — SEO brings traffic, AEO builds authority.

⚙️ CHAPTER 06 — Schema Markup — Structured Data for AEO & Rich Results

Schema Types to Implement on Each Page

Schema markup is JSON-LD code added to your website that tells Google and AI engines exactly what your content means. It powers rich results, knowledge panels, and AEO answer snippets.

💡 Use Google's Rich Results Test (search.google.com/test/rich-results) to validate schema after adding it to your site. FAQPage schema has the highest AEO impact for a service business.
