# Remote Marketing Services Page

## Purpose

The Remote Marketing Services page helps global buyers choose Zexa Media services by their immediate business need instead of by location or technical channel names.

## URL

`/remote-marketing-services`

## Buyer Need Groups

- Get leads
- Improve ads
- Fix tracking
- Automate follow-up
- Improve social presence
- Build brand trust

## SEO Role

This page supports global service discovery without confining Zexa Media to one city, state or country. It links to the main global commercial service pages and explains remote execution, service fit and buyer decision paths.

## Conversion Role

The page moves visitors toward a free audit when they are unsure which service to choose first. Each buyer-need card links to relevant service pages and includes a clear audit CTA.
