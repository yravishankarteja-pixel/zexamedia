# Zexa Media Review Strategy

## Current Status

Zexa Media has a consistent website NAP and Google Business Profile field plan. The next priority is to collect genuine client and partner reviews in a way that improves local trust without violating Google review policies.

## Review Goal

Build a steady base of honest reviews from real clients and partners who have directly worked with Zexa Media. The goal is credibility, not artificial volume.

Reviews should help prospects understand:

- What service Zexa Media provided.
- What communication and execution felt like.
- Whether strategy, ads, social media, branding, automation, landing pages, or tracking improved clarity.
- Why the client would recommend Zexa Media.

Do not ask reviewers to mention exact rankings, fake results, inflated numbers, or keywords unnaturally.

## Policy Rules

Follow these rules for every review request:

- Ask only people who have genuinely worked with Zexa Media.
- Ask for honest feedback, not positive feedback.
- Do not offer discounts, gifts, payment, free services, or priority support in exchange for reviews.
- Do not ask employees, owners, family members, or fake accounts to review the business.
- Do not ask only happy clients while excluding unhappy clients.
- Do not ask anyone to edit or remove a negative review in exchange for a benefit.
- Do not write reviews on behalf of customers.
- Do not copy the same review text for multiple customers.

## Google Review Link Setup

The business owner should create the direct Google review link from the verified Google Business Profile:

1. Open the Google Business Profile on a desktop browser.
2. Go to reviews.
3. Select the option to get more reviews.
4. Copy the review link or download the review QR code.
5. Save the link in the citation tracker.
6. Add the link to the website only after confirming it opens the Zexa Media review form.

Placeholder until the direct link is available:

```text
GOOGLE_REVIEW_LINK_TO_BE_ADDED_AFTER_GBP_VERIFICATION
```

## Review Request Timing

Best moments to request a review:

- After a successful campaign launch.
- After a client approves a landing page, ad account cleanup, content system, brand kit, or automation setup.
- After the first useful reporting or strategy review.
- After a client sends positive feedback on WhatsApp, email, or call.
- After a partner collaboration is completed.

Avoid asking during:

- Disputes.
- Payment follow-ups.
- Onboarding before any work is delivered.
- Moments where the client may feel pressured.

## Review Prompt For Clients

Do not give clients a scripted review. If they ask what to write, send this guidance:

```text
You can write whatever feels true from your experience. Helpful points could include what service we worked on, how communication was, what became clearer, and whether you would recommend Zexa Media to another business owner.
```

```text

```

Button:


Suggested placement:

- Footer, as a small secondary link near Contact.
- Thank-you page after form submission, if a thank-you page is created later.
- Email signature and WhatsApp quick reply, not as a pop-up.

## Review Response Templates

Positive review response:

```text
Thank you for sharing your experience with Zexa Media. We appreciate your trust and are glad the work was useful for your business.
```

Specific positive review response:

```text
Thank you for the thoughtful review. We enjoyed supporting your business with [service]. Clear communication, practical execution, and measurable growth are always our focus.
```

Neutral review response:

```text
Thank you for sharing your feedback. We appreciate the chance to work with you and will use your input to keep improving the Zexa Media experience.
```

Negative review response:

```text
Thank you for sharing your feedback. We are sorry your experience did not meet expectations. Please contact us at contactzexamedia@gmail.com or +91 95152 56630 so we can understand the issue and work toward a fair resolution.
```

Spam or fake review response:

```text
We could not match this review to a known client or partner interaction. We take feedback seriously and welcome the reviewer to contact us at contactzexamedia@gmail.com with more details.
```

## Monthly Review Routine

- Send 3 to 5 review requests to real eligible clients or partners.
- Respond to every new review within 3 business days.
- Screenshot useful reviews for internal records.
- Add review themes to service page copy only when they reflect real patterns.
- Do not overuse exact keywords in replies.
- Check for fake, spam, or mistaken reviews and report them through Google where appropriate.

## Website Trust Signals To Build Later

Once enough real reviews and testimonials exist:

- Add a testimonial section to the homepage.
- Add service-specific testimonials to service pages.
- Add a Reviews or Client Feedback section to the case studies page.
- Add review count or rating only if it is current and accurately sourced from Google.
- Mention ratings as being "on Google" and do not imply endorsement by Google.

## Verification Checklist

- Direct Google review link is created from the verified Business Profile.
- Review link opens the correct Zexa Media review form.
- WhatsApp and email templates use honest-review language.
- No incentive is offered.
- Review outreach is tracked.
- Public responses are professional and privacy-safe.
- Website review CTA is added only after the direct review link is confirmed.
