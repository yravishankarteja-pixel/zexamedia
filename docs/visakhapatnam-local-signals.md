# Visakhapatnam Local Landing Signals

## Current Status

The website now includes stronger city-level relevance signals on the homepage and growth-system page. Zexa Media is positioned as a real Visakhapatnam business serving Vizag, Andhra Pradesh, and clients across India.

## Problem

The site already mentioned Vizag and Visakhapatnam, but local signals were spread across pages. The homepage needed a clearer section that connects the business to:

- Dwaraka Nagar and GK Towers.
- Visakhapatnam service-area demand.
- Local buyer intent.
- Telugu-speaking audience context.
- Local industries such as clinics, education, wellness, real estate, and services.

## Exact Fix Implemented

- Added a homepage local-signals section titled `Built in Visakhapatnam, tuned for local demand`.
- Added references to Dwaraka Nagar, GK Towers, Kalanikethan Shopping Mall, MVP Colony, Rushikonda, Gajuwaka, Madhurawada, Andhra Pradesh, and Telugu-speaking audiences.
- Added internal CTA links from the local-signals cards to the contact page.
- Added homepage LocalBusiness schema fields for map URL and opening hours.
- Updated the growth-system page metadata to include Vizag and Visakhapatnam intent.
- Added a growth-system local-use-cases section for clinics, doctors, education, coaching, spas, wellness, real estate, and local services.

## Why This Matters

Local landing signals help users, search engines, map systems, and AI search systems understand that Zexa Media is not a generic national agency page. It is a Visakhapatnam-based marketing agency with local market context and service-area relevance.
